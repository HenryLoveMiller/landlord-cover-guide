How much is your property actually going to cost?

State averages are a starting point only. Your quote depends on the exact street, building materials, roof age, and your excess. Use the calculator for a closer estimate, then get real quotes.

What insurers look at when pricing your landlord policy

FactorWhat it means
What you coverBuilding only, contents only (carpets, appliances), or both. Building and contents together is usually cheaper than buying them separately.
Sum insuredThe higher the declared value of the building and contents, the higher the premium.
Property characteristicsBuild material, roof type, age and condition. Old or unusual materials raise the premium.
LocationPostcode matters. Areas prone to cyclone, flood, bushfire or high crime cost more.
Optional coverLoss of rent if a tenant defaults or breaks the lease, accidental damage, tenant pet damage — each adds to the premium.
Security featuresHome alarms, security screens and monitored systems can reduce the premium.
ExcessRaising your excess lowers the premium, but you pay more at claim time. A $1,000 excess is the basis for every figure on this site.
Claims historyA history of claims raises the premium at renewal.
Number of propertiesInsuring multiple properties with one insurer is often cheaper.

Why North Queensland is its own number

North Queensland is priced separately from the rest of the state, and the gap is stark: $4,482 for a house against $2,529 elsewhere in Queensland — a 77% jump from crossing the boundary.

The drivers are cyclone, monsoon flooding and wet-season roof damage. If you own in the tropics, get quotes that specifically address cyclone resilience of the roof rather than accepting the statewide average as representative.

House or unit? The gap is large

Strata units run a fraction of house costs because the body corporate insures the building. You are insuring contents and rental exposure.

Ways to reduce the premium

  • Buy online rather than through a branch — direct policies usually run cheaper.
  • Combine building and contents cover.
  • Raise the excess. Every dollar of extra excess takes money off the annual premium; the trade-off is what you pay per claim.
  • Install a monitored alarm, security screens and deadlocks. Insurers price these in.
  • Pay annually — monthly instalments usually carry a loading.
  • Quote at least three insurers every renewal, not just the first. Renewal hikes range from low single digits to over 40% on individual policies.
Note on tax. You may be able to claim landlord insurance premiums as a tax deduction in certain circumstances. Whether yours qualify is a question for a registered tax agent, not for this site.
