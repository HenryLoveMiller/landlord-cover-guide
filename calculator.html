How to read this

The calculator starts from your state's average annual premium, then adjusts for excess and optional cover. Rent affects the figure mainly because loss-of-rent cover is priced against the rent at risk. It is a directional estimate built on published averages — your quote will move with your exact address, building age and claims history.

Not a quote. This calculator uses state-level averages from a published research report. It cannot price your individual property. To get a real figure you need quotes from insurers or a licensed broker.
